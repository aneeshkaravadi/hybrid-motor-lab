# For the Impulse Space team

1. **Equilibrium rocket performance** (`hml/thermo.py`): c*, Isp at any area ratio, and frozen vs shifting expansion, from Cantera and NASA thermo data. Checked against textbook flame temperatures.
2. **Nozzle contour to printable CAD** (`hml/nozzle.py`), with a DfAM check of the exported part. ([report](dfam_report.md))
3. In the companion repo [`radiant-thermal`](https://github.com/aneeshkaravadi/radiant-thermal), radiator sizing for spacecraft heat rejection from coating absorptance and emissivity.

**A 3-week project I could do for you, remote:** a performance-trade tool for in-space engines (area ratio vs mass vs Isp) that exports nozzle CAD for each design point.
